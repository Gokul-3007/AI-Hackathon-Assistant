Problem Statement ID: PS-101
Title: AI-Based Crop Disease Detection
Organization: AgriFuture Labs
Domain: Agriculture
Description: Farmers struggle to identify crop diseases early, leading to yield loss and higher expenses. The solution should detect diseases from plant images and offer actionable recommendations.
Requirements: Image-based disease detection, multilingual support, recommendations for treatment, offline support for rural areas.
Expected Outcome: A working prototype that detects common crop diseases using AI and provides farmer-friendly guidance.
Constraints: Limited internet connectivity, low-quality images, need for rapid inference on mobile devices.

Problem Statement ID: PS-202
Title: Smart Student Career Guidance Platform
Organization: FutureSkills Foundation
Domain: Education
Description: Students need support in choosing skill pathways and career plans. The system should analyze student interests, skills, and market demand to recommend future-ready learning tracks.
Requirements: Personalized recommendations, career analytics, skill gap identification, dashboard for students and mentors.
Expected Outcome: A recommendation engine that helps students understand their strengths and pathways for growth.
Constraints: Data privacy, diverse student backgrounds, limited onboarding data.

Problem Statement ID: PS-303
Title: AI-Assisted Chronic Disease Monitoring
Organization: Public Health Innovation Unit
Domain: Healthcare
Description: Chronic disease patients often miss follow-up care because of limited physician capacity and poor monitoring. The system should alert healthcare teams when patient conditions worsen.
Requirements: Symptom tracking, AI-based risk prediction, clinician dashboard, medication adherence reminders.
Expected Outcome: Early intervention and improved patient care outcomes.
Constraints: Sensitive health data, compliance requirements, low digital literacy in some caregivers.

Problem Statement ID: PS-404
Title: AI-Powered Waste Segregation System
Organization: GreenCity Initiative
Domain: Sustainability
Description: Municipalities need reliable segregation of recyclable and organic waste. A solution can detect and classify waste types in real time.
Requirements: Image-based waste recognition, dashboard for municipal operations, integration with collection schedules.
Expected Outcome: Improved segregation efficiency and lower landfill waste.
Constraints: Diverse waste materials, outdoor lighting changes, deployment across multiple districts.
